Place it in the plugins folder (this step isn't necesary, but it is recommended so you don't re-distribute files you shouldn't share)

## Description
You're an assassin that lives in an abandoned mansion, one day, after some eliminations, you see 5 dwarves graffitiing your home. They see you holding a knife, scared, they get inside your house. Your mission? Eliminate them before the cops get here.

## Tutorial
Those damn dwarves are scared of you, and your job is to hunt them, they are hid in objects inside the house, so make sure to knife those things until they're scared and pop out!<br>
After a dwarf is killed, you can grab him and then drag him to a trash can, which are located in the first room you're in (the outside).<br>
When there's a "CRACK" it means thunder struck and the lights went out quickly, which gives the dwarfs a chance to escape the room.<br>

## Controls
- Arrow Keys || WASD : to move <br>
- Z : Attack <br>
- X : Grab
